CapEx refers to the one-time, upfront investment in physical assets or infrastructure that provides long-term value to the business.

**Examples:**

- Buying servers, networking equipment, or data centers.
- Purchasing software licenses
- Constructing office buildings or warehouses.

OpEx includes ongoing expenses required for the daily operation of a business. Instead of purchasing assets, companies "rent" or "subscribe" to services.

**Examples:**

- Cloud computing (AWS, Azure, GCP pay-as-you-go).
- SaaS software subscriptions (e.g., Microsoft 365, Zoom).
- Leasing office spaces or renting hardware.
- Salaries, utilities, and maintenance costs.
